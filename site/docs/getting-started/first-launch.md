# First Launch

Crater uses two windows:

- **The console** is where you work. It opens maximized on your main screen, and only you see it.
- **The projection window** is what the congregation sees. It goes on your projector, TV or second monitor.

A few minutes of setup gets the projection window onto the right screen.

## Step 1: Connect your projector or second screen

1. Plug the projector or TV into the computer (HDMI, DisplayPort, USB-C or a VGA adapter) and turn it on.
2. Set the computer to **extend** the desktop across both screens:
    - **Windows:** press **Win + P** and choose **Extend**.
    - **macOS:** open **System Settings > Displays** and make sure the second display isn't set to mirror.

You can plug the projector in before or after starting Crater. Crater notices when screens are connected or removed.

## Step 2: Choose the projection screen

On a fresh install Crater picks your first non-main screen automatically. To check or change it:

1. Click the small arrow on the right of the **Go Live** button in the top-right corner.
2. The menu lists every connected screen by name and resolution. Your main screen is marked **(primary)**. Click the one connected to your projector.
3. In the same menu, choose **Fullscreen** (normal for a projector) or **Windowed** (a movable window, handy for testing).

The same options are in **Settings > Projection** under **Output display** and **Projection mode**.

## Step 3: Open the projection window

Click **Go Live** in the top bar. The projection window opens on the screen you chose and the button changes to **End Live**. Click **End Live** to close it again.

## Step 4: Run a quick test

1. Click the **Scripture** tab in the bottom half of the console.
2. Type `jn 3:16` in the search box on the left. Crater jumps to John 3:16 and shows it in the **Preview** panel.
3. Press **Enter**. The verse goes to the **Live** panel and onto the projector.
4. Click **Clear** in the top bar to hide the text, and click it again to bring it back.

If the verse appears on the projector, you're ready. [Running a Service](../guides/displaying-content.md) explains the rest of the workflow.

## Using Crater with only one screen

Crater works on a single laptop screen too, which is useful for preparing a service at home.

- By default, the projection shows as a **small preview window in the bottom-right corner** of your screen.
- For a full-size view, turn on **Settings > Projection > Single display: full-size projection behind the console**. The projection then fills the screen behind every other window. Snap the console to one half of the screen (**Win + Left arrow** on Windows) to see both side by side.

When you plug a second screen in later, check it's selected in the menu next to **Go Live**.

## Optional: make the console your own

- **Language.** Crater picks your computer's language the first time it runs, if it has a translation for it. Change it any time in **Settings > Appearance > Language**. The switch happens straight away, with no restart. See [Settings](../reference/settings.md#appearance) for the list of 21 languages.
- **Console colours.** **Settings > Appearance > Theme** offers Dark, Light, Midnight, Auto (follows your computer) and eleven more, including High Contrast and Sepia. This only changes the console. The projection keeps its own look.
- **Text size.** **Settings > Appearance > Font size** makes the console text smaller (S) or larger (L).
- **Default Bible.** Crater opens the Scripture tab in the King James Version. Pick another in **Settings > Scripture > Default version**.

## Common setup problems

**The projection window opened on the wrong screen.** Pick the right screen from the arrow next to **Go Live**. Crater remembers screens by name, so it finds the projector again even if you plug it into a different port next week.

**My projector isn't in the list.** Check the computer sees it first (Win + P on Windows, System Settings > Displays on a Mac). Crater updates its list as soon as the operating system reports the new screen.

**I only see a small window in the corner.** Crater thinks there's only one screen. See [Using Crater with only one screen](#using-crater-with-only-one-screen), or check the projector is connected and set to extend.

More fixes are in [Troubleshooting](../reference/troubleshooting.md).
